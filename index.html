The right team for the right roof

Two Roofing Divisions. Distinct Areas of Expertise.

Residential roofing and commercial roofing have different systems, priorities and project requirements. Our two divisions are organized around those differences, so homeowners and property professionals can reach the team focused on their type of roof.

TRD · Residential Division

The Roof Doctors

TRD helps homeowners understand the condition of their roof and choose practical next steps. Work begins with a careful evaluation, clear findings and recommendations based on the home, roof system and observed conditions.

  • Free 21-point roof inspections
  • Roof leak diagnosis and repairs
  • Residential roof replacement
  • Hail and wind damage assessments
  • Storm restoration documentation
  • Emergency tarping and response
  • Attic, ventilation and moisture checks
  • Roofing materials and system guidance

Residential service coverage

Based in Texarkana, TRD's core residential coverage generally extends about 75 miles from the Texarkana area. Core communities include Texarkana, Atlanta, New Boston, Marshall and Longview. Requests from extended-area communities such as Paris, Sulphur Springs and Tyler are reviewed individually based on project scope and scheduling.

CRD · Commercial Division

Commercial Roof Doctors

CRD works with building owners, facility teams and property managers to inspect, repair, maintain, restore and plan commercial roof systems. Recommendations account for roof condition, building use, drainage, system compatibility and long-term property needs.

  • Commercial roof inspections and diagnostics
  • Leak tracing and commercial roof repairs
  • Preventative maintenance programs
  • TPO, EPDM, PVC and modified bitumen
  • Commercial metal roof repair and renewal
  • Silicone, acrylic and polyurethane coatings
  • Roof restoration and replacement planning
  • Multi-property roof asset management
  • Storm documentation and response
  • Emergency water-intrusion mitigation

Commercial service coverage

Based in Texarkana, CRD considers commercial, industrial and multi-site projects across Texas, Arkansas, Louisiana and Oklahoma. Travel, mobilization and availability are evaluated for each project based on property location, roof system, scope and schedule. Coverage is confirmed during consultation.

Roofing division questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between TRD and CRD?

The Roof Doctors (TRD) focuses on residential roofing for homeowners in Northeast Texas. Commercial Roof Doctors (CRD) focuses on commercial and industrial roofing systems, including inspections, repairs, maintenance, coatings and restoration across Texas, Arkansas, Louisiana and Oklahoma.

What residential roofing services does TRD provide?

TRD provides roof inspections, leak diagnosis and repairs, roof replacement, hail and wind damage assessments, storm restoration documentation, emergency tarping and related residential roofing services.

Which communities does TRD serve?

TRD is based in Texarkana. Core residential communities include Texarkana, Atlanta, New Boston, Marshall and Longview, with core coverage generally extending about 75 miles from Texarkana. Requests from Paris, Sulphur Springs and Tyler are reviewed individually according to scope and scheduling.

What types of commercial roofing does CRD work on?

CRD works with commercial roof systems including TPO, EPDM, PVC, modified bitumen and metal roofing. Services include inspections, leak tracing, repairs, maintenance, coatings, restoration and replacement planning.

Does CRD serve commercial properties outside Texas?

CRD considers commercial and industrial projects in Texas, Arkansas, Louisiana and Oklahoma. Availability and mobilization are confirmed for each property and project scope.

How do I contact the right division?
