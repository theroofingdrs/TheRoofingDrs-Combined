## Residential (The ROOF Doctors)
Use residential assets for the residential home page, gallery, roof replacement, roof repair, storm damage, inspection, metal roofing and roofing systems pages.

## Commercial (Commercial ROOF Doctors)
Use commercial assets for the commercial home page, commercial roofing overview, coatings and restoration, TPO, EPDM, modified bitumen, metal roofing, inspections, maintenance and replacement pages. Prioritize coatings/restoration content in CRD page imagery.

## Multi-category reuse

Each original is retained once. Use the manifest tags and additional_placements to reuse that same website asset across relevant service, roof-type, building-type and industry pages; do not create a separate image copy for each placement.

Examples include barn imagery across agricultural reroofing and metal-roof replacement, and warehouse imagery across industrial roofing, storage-building coatings, silicone restoration and maintenance.

## Visual truth

Use images as illustrative service imagery unless a specific completed customer project is independently verified. Do not imply a customer relationship, address, project result, testimonial or endorsement from an image alone.

### Video publishing rules

- Keep the original MP4 files in the incoming archive. Create optimized web derivatives only after reviewing source quality and duration.
- Prefer click-to-play with a poster image, captions or a concise transcript, and a visible play control. Avoid autoplay with sound and avoid making video a prerequisite for page content.
- Do not publish a timelapse as proof of a specific installation method, warranty, energy savings, or project outcome unless the footage and supporting records establish that claim.
- Use a page-specific poster image from the approved image library. Keep the correct CRD logo treatment separate from footage unless the source video already contains an approved brand treatment.
- Do not claim a customer, location, project date, or facility relationship from recognizable buildings or signage without confirmation.
- The agricultural and storage-center page placements are future allocations, not claims that those pages currently exist.
